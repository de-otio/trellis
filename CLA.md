grants those rights. **It does not transfer your copyright** — you retain

## How to sign

Contributions are gated by the [CLA Assistant](https://github.com/cla-assistant/cla-assistant)
bot. When you open your first pull request, the bot will post a link; signing is
a one-time action recorded against your GitHub account and covers all future
pull requests. Pull requests cannot be merged until the CLA is signed.
