# Problem Statement

## The "Forever" Problem

UK adults are increasingly self-censoring on social media due to fear that old posts will resurface and damage their careers or reputations. According to Ofcom's 2025 Adults' Media Use and Attitudes report (survey of 7,500 people aged 16+):

- Active posting/sharing/commenting dropped from 61% to 49% of adult social media users
- The proportion of adults worried that old posts could cause future problems rose from 43% (2024) to 49% (2025)
- Users who believe the benefits of being online outweigh the risks fell from 72% to 59%
- Some users have stopped posting entirely or switched to temporary formats like Instagram Stories

**Source:** The Guardian / Ofcom, 2 April 2026

## Core User Need

Users want relief from the anxiety that a throwaway post at 22 will torpedo a job interview at 32. They don't need NSA-grade forward secrecy -- they need to make old content practically inaccessible through normal channels.
