## Why Now

1. **Creator economy inflection**: Brand spending shifting from macro-influencers to authentic micro/nano content. Trellis's value-exchange model is built for this shift.
2. **Pet industry digitization**: DACH pet e-commerce growing 15-20% annually. Brands need digital community channels.
3. **Trust crisis in social media**: Users and regulators pushing back on attention-extraction models. A transparent, non-addictive alternative has cultural tailwind.
4. **UGC proven ROI**: Bazaarvoice data shows 144% conversion lift from UGC. Brands have budget for this; they just need the platform.
5. **DACH regulatory advantage**: Strict GDPR/consumer protection forces good habits early, creating a compliance moat that's hard for less privacy-conscious competitors to replicate.
