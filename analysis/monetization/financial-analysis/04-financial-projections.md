# Financial Projections: 5-Year Model

Three scenarios with clearly stated assumption differences. Year 1 is monthly, Year 2 is quarterly, Years 3-5 are annual. All figures in USD.

---

## User Growth Assumptions

### Monthly Active Users (MAU)

| Period | Conservative | Base | Optimistic | Growth Driver |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Year 1 end | 8,000 | 12,000 | 18,000 | Organic community building, existing Trellis users |
| Year 2 end | 30,000 | 55,000 | 90,000 | Value-exchange launch, brand partnerships, word of mouth |
| Year 3 end | 80,000 | 160,000 | 300,000 | Self-serve brand portal, content marketing, referrals |
| Year 4 end | 180,000 | 420,000 | 700,000 | International expansion (broader EU), paid acquisition |
| Year 5 end | 350,000 | 850,000 | 1,500,000 | Market leadership in DACH, US entry |

Growth rate benchmarks:
- Series A bar (a16z/Sequoia): 15-20% MoM sustained over 6+ months
- Base case Year 1: ~15% MoM (organic community); Year 2: ~13% MoM (growth phase)

| Segment | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 | Year 4 | Year 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paid subscribers | 1% | 4% | 5% | 6% | 6.5% |

Year 1 has no value-exchange (social-first phase). Conversion improves as premium features mature and brand content quality increases.

## Important Caveats

1. **Year 1 is pre-revenue investment**. The $113K burn assumes a single developer with minimal costs. Any team expansion accelerates burn.
2. **Net margins above 80% are unlikely to sustain** at scale. As the company grows, costs for compliance, moderation, customer support, and organizational overhead will compress margins toward 50-70%.
3. **Brand partnership revenue assumes successful product-market fit** on the brand side. If brands don't see ROI from value actions, this stream underperforms.
4. **These projections exclude one-time costs**: legal setup, incorporation, initial brand acquisition campaigns, potential app store review processes.
5. **Currency risk**: DACH revenue in EUR, costs partially in USD (AWS). Exchange rate fluctuations affect margins.
