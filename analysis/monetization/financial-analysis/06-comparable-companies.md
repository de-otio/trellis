## Valuation Frameworks Investors Will Apply

### 1. Per-DAU Valuation (Pre-Revenue / Early Revenue)

| Benchmark | $/DAU | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Typical Series A consumer social | $20-100 | a16z, Sequoia frameworks |
| Discord (at peak valuation) | ~$250/DAU | $15B / ~60M DAU |
| Reddit (IPO) | ~$35/DAU | $6.5B IPO / ~100M+ DAU |
| Nextdoor (IPO) | ~$540/DAU | $4.3B / ~8M DAU (inflated) |

For Trellis at Year 2 end (55K MAU, ~14K DAU at 25% DAU/MAU):
- At $50/DAU: **$700K valuation** (too early for this metric)
- At $100/DAU: **$1.4M valuation**

Per-DAU is most useful pre-revenue. Once revenue exists, revenue multiples dominate.

### 2. Revenue Multiple (Post-Revenue)

Primary valuation method from Series A onward. Multiple is a function of:
- Growth rate (higher growth = higher multiple)
- Gross margin (>70% is expected for software)
- Net retention (>100% commands premium)
- Market size (larger TAM = higher multiple)

### 3. Comparable Transaction

Most relevant for fundraising. "Company X raised at Y valuation with Z metrics; we have comparable/better metrics in an adjacent market."

**Best comp for Trellis**: AllTrails or Strava (niche outdoor/fitness community with freemium subscription, valued at 4-5x revenue at scale).

## Competitive Landscape

### Direct Competitors (Pet Social)

| Platform | Status | Differentiation from Trellis |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram/TikTok pet communities | Thriving but unstructured | General-purpose; no pet-specific tools or value-exchange |
| Dogster/Catster | Legacy, low engagement | Outdated UX; content-driven not community-driven |

### Indirect Competitors

| Platform | Threat Level | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | Medium | Default pet content platform; but no pet-specific utility |
| TikTok | Medium | Discovery-driven; but no community depth |
| Facebook Groups | Low-Medium | Existing pet groups; but declining platform trust |
| Nextdoor (pet features) | Low | Hyperlocal; limited pet-specific functionality |

### Trellis's Competitive Moat

1. **Vertical depth**: Pet health tracking, breed-specific features, vet integration — utility features general platforms won't build
2. **Community identity**: Dog-fan identity creates emotional switching cost
3. **Value-exchange model**: Unique monetization that no competitor offers — users earn from participation
4. **Data advantage**: Pet health and behavior data at scale is valuable and defensible
5. **DACH regulatory compliance**: Built for strict EU privacy from day one; hard for US-first platforms to replicate
6. **Transparency positioning**: Non-addictive, transparent design is a brand differentiator in the current social media trust crisis

## Investor Positioning

### Narrative for Different Investor Types

**Angel / Pre-Seed**: "A social platform for dog lovers in the DACH region with a novel monetization model where users earn from brands they already like. We've built the product, have an existing community, and need $120-150K to reach profitability."

**Seed**: "We've proven the community engagement model with [X] MAU and [Y]% retention. The value-exchange monetization is live with [Z] brand partners generating $[N] MRR. We're raising to accelerate user growth and brand acquisition."

**Series A**: "We're the leading dog-fan community in DACH with [X]K MAU, $[N]K MRR, and a value-exchange model generating 4.7:1 LTV:CAC. We're raising to expand across the EU and build the self-serve brand platform."

### What Makes This Fundable

1. **Low capital requirement**: Self-sustaining with <$150K pre-seed
2. **Multiple revenue streams**: Not dependent on any single source
3. **Strong unit economics**: 4.7:1 LTV:CAC (base case) exceeds most consumer benchmarks
4. **Large TAM with clear SOM path**: DACH pet market is EUR 14-18B; 5-15% penetration of digital-active dog owners is achievable
5. **Novel but credible model**: Value-exchange is differentiated but grounded in proven economics (cashback platforms, UGC marketplaces)
6. **Mission alignment**: Non-addictive, transparent design appeals to impact-focused investors
