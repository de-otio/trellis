# Quantified Risk

Revenue concentration data and impact analysis for brand dependency.

---

## Brand Revenue as Share of Total Revenue

| Year | Total Revenue (Monthly) | Brand VE Revenue (Monthly) | Brand % of Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Year 1 | $0.75K | $0 | 0% |
| Year 2 | $41K | $19.4K | 46% |
| Year 3 | $247K | $115.5K | 47% |
| Year 4 | $696K | $353K | 51% |
| Year 5 | $1,693K | $882K | 52% |

Brand revenue share grows slightly over time because value-exchange revenue scales with both user base and brand count. This is not alarming — 52% at scale with 300 brands is very different from 46% with 35 brands — but it means brand revenue management is a permanent concern, not a temporary one.

## Brand Revenue Concentration Over Time

| Year | Brand Partners | Top Brand % of Brand Revenue | Top 3 Brands % | Top 10 Brands % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Year 1 | 5-10 | 30-40% | 70-80% | 100% |
| Year 2 | 35 | 15-20% | 40-50% | 70-80% |
| Year 3 | 75 | 10-12% | 25-30% | 50-60% |
| Year 4 | 150 | 5-8% | 15-20% | 35-45% |
| Year 5 | 300 | 3-5% | 10-15% | 25-35% |

**Year 1**: Irrelevant — brand revenue is ~$0 (social-first phase).

**Year 2**: Highest risk. 35 brands, but revenue is concentrated. Top brand could be 20% of brand revenue = 9% of total revenue. Losing them hurts.

**Year 3+**: Natural diversification reduces concentration. By Year 5, top brand is 3-5% of brand revenue = 1.5-2.6% of total. Loss is manageable.

## Vulnerability Windows

### Year 2 Q1-Q2: Maximum Vulnerability

- Brand revenue is becoming significant (~46% of total)
- Brand count is still low (15-25 brands)
- Self-serve portal not yet live (brands acquired through direct sales)
- Replacement pipeline is thin
- Cash reserves from pre-seed are nearly depleted

**Mitigation**: Ensure founding brand partnerships have 12-month minimum commitments. Maintain $15K cash buffer (2 months of top-brand revenue). Accelerate self-serve portal launch to reduce dependence on direct sales.

### Year 2 Q3-Q4: Declining Vulnerability

- Brand count reaches 35+
- Self-serve portal is live
- Replacement pipeline is building
- Other revenue streams are growing

### Year 3+: Manageable

- 75+ brands, growing toward 300
- Natural diversification reduces single-brand leverage
- Self-serve portal enables rapid replacement
- Cash position is strong (cumulative positive from Year 2)

## Stress Test: What If Brand Revenue Underperforms?

If brand value-exchange revenue comes in 30% below base case (due to lower participation OR lower brand payments OR fewer brands):

| Year | Base Case Total | Stress Case Total | Difference | Still Profitable? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Year 2 | $41K/month | $35K/month | -15% | Yes (marginally) |
| Year 3 | $247K/month | $212K/month | -14% | Yes |
| Year 5 | $1,693K/month | $1,429K/month | -16% | Yes |

**The model survives a 30% brand revenue shortfall at all stages.** This is because the other three streams (subscriptions, ambient, B2B tools) provide a meaningful floor. The platform would grow more slowly but remain profitable.

## Concentration Limit Enforcement

### Recommended Thresholds

| Metric | Yellow Alert | Red Alert | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single brand % of brand revenue | >15% | >25% | Actively recruit competitors |
| Top 3 brands % of brand revenue | >40% | >55% | Diversification sprint |
| Brand revenue % of total revenue | >50% | >60% | Invest in subscription/B2B growth |
| Brand partner count | <20 | <10 | Pause brand onboarding expansion; focus on retention |

### Monitoring Cadence

- **Monthly**: Check single-brand and top-3 concentration
- **Quarterly**: Review brand revenue % of total, publish in transparency report
- **Annually**: Full brand portfolio review — category diversity, geographic diversity, contract renewal pipeline
