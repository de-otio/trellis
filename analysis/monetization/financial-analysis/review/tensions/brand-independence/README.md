# Brand Revenue Dependency vs. Editorial Independence

**Risk level**: Medium — manageable at launch but requires structural safeguards that scale.

This is one of the most critical tensions in the monetization model. When nearly half of revenue comes from brand payments, brands have implicit leverage — even without making explicit threats. This is the same structural force that made ad-supported social platforms optimize for advertisers over users.

The value-exchange model was designed to avoid this by making brands pay for authentic user behavior rather than attention. But authenticity is exactly what's at risk when brand revenue dominates.

## Summary of Recommended Commitments

1. Quality thresholds are platform-wide and brand-agnostic — no per-brand exceptions, ever
2. Action types are platform-defined — brands can request, not require
3. Users choose action types, not brands — brand budgets cover all types equally
4. Concentration limit: no brand >20% of brand revenue — monitored quarterly
5. Editorial decisions are structurally separated from brand revenue responsibility
6. Brand partnership agreements include editorial independence clause
