# Value Actions

Users "pay" for the platform by performing micro-contributions that generate real business value for brands. The brand pays the platform per completed action; the user earns continued platform access through these contributions.

---

## Action Types

### Authentic Product Reviews

Short, honest assessments from real users. These are not prompted to be positive — authenticity is the selling point.

- Structured format (rating + short text + optional photo)
- Verified purchase or verified brand relationship required
- Review quality scoring to prevent low-effort submissions

### Peer Q&A

Answering real questions from prospective buyers.

- Example: *"Is this jacket actually warm enough for Berlin winters?"*
- Questions sourced from brand websites, product pages, or community
- Answers are attributed and labeled as value actions

### Product Feedback Surveys

Quick, structured input that brands actually use for product development.

- Short (2-5 minutes maximum)
- Focused on genuine product improvement, not marketing disguised as research
- Results aggregated and anonymized before delivery to brands

### User-Endorsed Recommendations

Genuinely useful posts clearly tagged as endorsements, never disguised as organic content.

- User chooses which brands/products to endorse
- Displayed with clear labeling in all contexts
- Limited frequency to prevent feed saturation

---

## Design Constraints

- **Weekly cap**: Users should never feel like the platform is a job. A cap on value actions per week (e.g., 3-5 actions) keeps the contribution lightweight.
- **Choice**: Users always choose which brands and which action types. No forced assignments.
- **Quality over quantity**: Reward thoughtful contributions, not volume. A single well-written review should count more than five rushed ones.
- **Opt-out path**: Users who don't want to do value actions can pay a monthly fee instead (see [06-revenue-model.md](06-revenue-model.md)).

- How do we score "quality" without introducing subjective editorial judgment?

- Can value actions extend beyond brand interactions (e.g., community moderation, content curation)?
