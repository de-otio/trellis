# Brand-Side Economics

An authentic, considered endorsement from a real user who actually chose that brand is high-conversion content. It's word-of-mouth at scale, with structure and traceability. This is vastly more valuable than a display ad.

---

## Why Brands Would Pay

### Compared to Display Advertising

| Factor | Display Ads | Value Actions |
|---|---|---|
| Trust | Low (ad blindness, ad blockers) | High (real user, chosen freely) |
| Conversion | ~0.1-0.5% CTR | Higher — peer endorsement drives purchase intent |
| Content lifespan | Seconds (scroll past) | Persistent (reviews, Q&A stay discoverable) |
| Brand safety | Unpredictable placement | Controlled, user-initiated context |
| Data quality | Inferred from tracking | Explicit, first-party, consensual |

### Compared to Influencer Marketing

- Lower cost per action than influencer campaigns
- Higher authenticity — real users, not professional promoters
- Scalable across thousands of micro-contributors vs. a handful of influencers
- More predictable pricing (per-action vs. per-post negotiation)

---

## Pricing Model

### Per-Action Pricing

Brands pay per verified, completed value action:

| Action Type | Suggested Price Range | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Product review | $2-5 per review | High value, persistent content |
| Peer Q&A answer | $1-3 per answer | Lower effort, high conversion value |
| Feedback survey | $0.50-2 per response | Structured data, aggregated delivery |
| Endorsed recommendation | $3-8 per post | Highest visibility, social proof |

### Platform Take Rate

- Platform retains 30-50% of brand payment
- Remainder credited to user's "value balance" (access credits, not cash)
- Tiered pricing for high-volume brand partners

### Volume Commitments

- Brands can purchase action bundles at discounted rates
- Minimum monthly commitment for priority placement in user action queues
- Enterprise pricing for brands wanting exclusive category presence

---

## Brand Onboarding

- Self-serve portal for small brands (set budget, define action types, launch)
- Managed onboarding for enterprise brands (custom action design, dedicated support)
- Brand verification process to prevent fraudulent or harmful brands

## Open Questions

- What's the break-even point: how many active users needed per brand to justify the cost?
- How do we handle brands that want to pay for positive-only reviews? (Answer: we don't — but what's the enforcement mechanism?)
- Should brands see individual user data, or only aggregated results?
- How do we price across markets with different purchasing power (e.g., DACH vs. Southeast Asia)?
